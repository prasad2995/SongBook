Song 61

1.We praise Thee O God!
for the Son of Thy love,
For Jesus who died,
and is now gone above.

Hallelujah! Thine the glory,
Hallelujah! Amen;
Hallelujah! Thine the glory,
Revive us again.

2.We praise Thee, O God!
for Thy spirit of light,
Who has shown us our Saviour,
and has scattered our night.

3.All glory and praise to the Lamb
that was slain,
Who has borne all our sins, and
has cleansed ev’ry stain.

4.Revive us again; fill each heart with Thy love;
May each soul be rekindled with
fire from above.
