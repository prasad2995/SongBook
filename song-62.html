Song 62

1.With harps and with vials there
stand a great throng
In the presence of Jesus,
and sing this new song:

Unto Him who hath loved us and
washed us from sin,
Unto Him be the glory for ever! Amen.

2. All these once were sinners,
defiled in His sight;
Now arrayed in pure garments in
praise they unite:

3.He maketh the rebel a priest and
a king;
He hath bought us, and taught us
this new song to sing:

4.How helpless and hopeless we
sinners had been
If he never had loved us till
cleansed from our sin!

5.Aloud in His praises our voices
shall ring,
So that others, believing, this new
song shall sing.
